Meet Your Artist

Ink by Raj

Clean lines. Honest advice. A tattoo you’ll still love in twenty years.

Raj is the artist behind The North Ink in Whitehorse. The studio sits inside North Style Unisex Salon on 4th Ave — a calm, clean, private setup where every piece gets proper time, from the first consult to final aftercare advice.

  • Custom drawing for every non-flash piece — no repeats
